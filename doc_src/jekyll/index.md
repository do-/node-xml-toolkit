# Why node-xml-toolkit?

Picture yourself working on a large Node.js project, where
- part of your tasks is to invoke dozens SOAP services with WSDLs complex enough to kill any hope to treat messages just as strings,
- another one is to load a multi GB XML data exports into a database, unobtrusively reporting on inconsistencies with a given XML Schema,
- on top of that, you need to send back your dumps in similar format.
